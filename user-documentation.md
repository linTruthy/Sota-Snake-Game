## Getting Started

### First Launch
1. Download the game from your device's app store
2. Launch the game
3. You'll be prompted to enter a username
   - Choose a unique username (3-20 characters)
   - Only letters, numbers, and underscores are allowed
   - This will be your identity on the leaderboards

### Basic Navigation
- **Main Menu**: Access game modes, leaderboards, and settings
- **Settings**: Adjust sound, music, and display preferences
- **Profile**: View your stats and change username
- **Leaderboards**: Check global and weekly rankings
- **Daily Challenges**: View and complete daily tasks

## Game Basics

### Objective
Guide your snake to eat food while avoiding:
- Colliding with walls
- Running into your own tail
- Crashing into obstacles

### Level Progression
- Each game starts at Level 1
- Score 100 points to advance to the next level
- Snake speed increases with each level
- Higher levels offer more points per food item

### Game Over Conditions
- Collision with your own tail
- Hitting walls (unless you have invincibility)
- Running out of time (in timed modes)

## Controls

### Touch Controls
- **Swipe Up**: Direct snake upward
- **Swipe Down**: Direct snake downward
- **Swipe Left**: Direct snake left
- **Swipe Right**: Direct snake right

### Additional Controls
- **Pause Button**: Center-bottom of screen
- **Sound Toggle**: Top-right corner
- **Menu Button**: Access game menu

## Power-Ups

### Types of Power-Ups
1. **Speed Boost**
   - Increases snake movement speed
   - Duration: 30 seconds
   - Indicated by yellow lightning icon

2. **Score Multiplier**
   - Doubles points earned
   - Duration: 30 seconds
   - Indicated by star icon

3. **Invincibility**
   - Prevents death from collisions
   - Duration: 30 seconds
   - Indicated by shield icon

4. **Slow Motion**
   - Reduces snake speed
   - Duration: 30 seconds
   - Indicated by clock icon

### Power-Up Tips
- Power-ups appear randomly on the game board
- Multiple power-ups can't be active simultaneously
- Progress bar shows remaining power-up duration
- Collect power-ups by running over them with your snake

## Scoring System

### Point Values
- Regular Food: 10 points × current level
- With Score Multiplier: 20 points × current level

### Bonuses
- Level Completion: 50 bonus points
- Power-Up Collection: 25 bonus points
- Achievement Completion: Varies by achievement

### High Scores
- Local high score saved on device
- Global high scores displayed on leaderboard
- Weekly rankings reset every Sunday at midnight

## Daily Challenges

### Overview
- Three new challenges available each day
- Challenges reset at midnight
- Various types of objectives

### Challenge Types
1. **Score Challenges**
   - Reach specific score targets
   - Higher rewards for higher targets

2. **Survival Challenges**
   - Stay alive for specified duration
   - Increasing difficulty levels

3. **Collection Challenges**
   - Gather specific number of power-ups
   - Eat certain amount of food

### Rewards
- Points added to your total score
- Special achievements for completing challenges
- Progress tracked automatically

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

1. **Game Won't Load**
   - Check internet connection
   - Restart the app
   - Update to latest version

2. **Score Not Saving**
   - Verify internet connection
   - Check if logged in properly
   - Contact support if persistent

3. **Power-Ups Not Working**
   - Ensure game is updated
   - Check if power-up bar is visible
   - Restart the game
